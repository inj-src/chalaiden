# Home surface

Scope: the app's Home screen (`HomeScreen.kt`). Mode: Operate. Audience: everyday bKash users at a shop counter, one-handed, in a hurry. Task: start Pay, Send, Cash out or Balance; learn to scan the number (not the QR) on first use; pick the SIM on dual-SIM phones; reach Setup from the menu.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Chalaiden is the action hero, and Home is its hand-painted cinema poster, "now running". It refuses the fintech home (grey card, balance hero, icon row) and the meme-sticker opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Dhallywood hand-painted poster. Poster vermilion field (#D42A1F) over night indigo (#1B1446); marigold (#FFC21A) titles with a deep painted extrusion (#2A0D0A); cream (#FFE9C7) body ink; indigo-raised tiles (#2C2468).
